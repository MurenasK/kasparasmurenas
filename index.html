About Me

Hello — I'm Kasparas Murėnas, a student at Kauno technologijos universitetas (KTU) in Kaunas, Lithuania. I study Software Engineering and am drawn to building things that are elegant, fast, and purposeful.

Outside the lecture hall you'll find me deep in a forest, map in hand, racing across Europe. I've competed at the Junior World Championships three years in a row and made my senior World Cup debut in 2026 — orienteering taught me to think fast, navigate uncertainty, and push past limits.

This portfolio is a window into who I am — athlete, developer, maker, and explorer. I write code, I run through forests, and I'm always looking for the next problem to solve.
